1. Before Login (Landing Experience)
The user should never be presented with a static login screen. Instead, the experience should immediately feel like entering a professional AI-powered trading platform.
Background
Deep navy and black gradient.
Animated floating particles.
Soft moving light streaks.
Subtle glass reflections.
Slow ambient glow that moves across the screen.
Logo
The MaziwaTrader logo appears in the center with:
Soft cyan outer glow.
Gentle breathing animation.
Slight 3D floating effect.
Neon reflection underneath.
Login Button
When the Login button is pressed:
Button shrinks slightly as if being physically pressed.
Cyan glow expands around it.
Small ripple animation spreads outward.
Button changes into a loading state.
Loading spinner appears inside the button.
Button text smoothly changes from:
Login
to
Authenticating...
No page reload should be visible.
Register Button
When the Register button is pressed:
Same smooth press animation.
Purple-to-cyan glow.
Ripple animation.
Glass hover effect.
Opens registration smoothly with a fade transition.
After Login (Loading Animation)
Once authentication succeeds, the login page should not disappear instantly.
Instead...
The whole interface slowly fades into an AI loading screen.
AI Loading Screen
Dark futuristic background.
Center:
MaziwaTrader logo glowing.
Under the logo:
Animated status text changing every second.
Example:
Connecting to Deriv...
↓
Authenticating account...
↓
Loading market data...
↓
Syncing trading bots...
↓
Building dashboard...
↓
Analyzing volatility...
↓
Preparing AI modules...
↓
Loading completed...
Progress Bar
Not an ordinary progress bar.
Use:
Neon cyan line.
Light travelling through the line.
Percentage increasing smoothly.
0%
↓
18%
↓
36%
↓
58%
↓
82%
↓
100%
No jumps.
Number Animation
Behind the logo:
Trading digits constantly changing.
Examples
4 8 2
↓
7 3 9
↓
5 5 1
↓
8 1 6
Like live market ticks.
Final Transition
At 100%
Everything softly brightens.
Logo slightly enlarges.
Entire loading screen fades into Dashboard.
No flickering.
No white flash.
Only one continuous cinematic transition.
Top Toolbar
When clicking:
Dashboard
Bots
Bulk Trader
Analysis
Reports
Charts
Settings
Each menu should respond like a premium trading terminal.
Active Menu Animation
When clicked:
Previous menu loses glow smoothly.
New menu:
Slides slightly upward.
Gets surrounded by a glowing cyan pill.
Icon becomes brighter.
Text changes to white.
Outer glow expands softly.
Small pulse animation.
Glow remains until another menu is selected.
Glow Style
Not a flat blue.
Use:
Electric cyan.
Soft blur.
Glass reflection.
Small bloom effect.
Looks similar to premium fintech dashboards.
Page Transition
After clicking a toolbar item:
Old page fades to 90%.
New page slides upward about 10px.
Opacity:
0%
↓
100%
Duration:
250–350ms.
Feels like a desktop application.
Left Sidebar
The sidebar should feel alive.
When selecting:
Dashboard
Bot Builder
Analysis
Bulk Trader
Settings
etc.
Sidebar Animation
Clicked item:
Moves slightly inward.
Rounded glass background appears.
Blue neon glow spreads behind it.
Left glowing indicator bar slides into place.
Icon scales slightly larger.
Text brightens.
Everything animates smoothly.
Inactive menus:
Return to dark gray.
Glow disappears gradually.
No instant changes.
Sidebar Opening
When opening the sidebar:
Entire panel slides in smoothly.
Background blur appears.
Icons fade in one after another.
Looks premium.
Sidebar Closing
Slides left.
Opacity decreases.
Main content expands smoothly.
Overall Feel
The entire platform should feel like a high-end AI trading terminal rather than a standard website.
Every interaction should be:
Smooth.
Responsive.
Glassmorphism-based.
Neon cyan illuminated.
Consistent across login, loading, toolbar, sidebar, and page transitions.
The goal is to create an experience similar to a professional fintech application, where every click and transition feels polished, fluid, and visually premium.
